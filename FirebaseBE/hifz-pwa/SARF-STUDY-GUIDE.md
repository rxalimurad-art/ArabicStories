## 🔥 The 3 Main Families (Fa'ala Pattern)

These are the MOST IMPORTANT - most verbs use these patterns.

### Pattern 1: فَعَلَ / يَفْعَلُ (Past 'a', Present 'a')
| Form | Example | Meaning |
|------|---------|---------|
| Past | سَلَمَ | He was safe |
| Present | يَسْلَمُ | He is safe |
| Ism Fa'il | مُسْلِم | Muslim (doer) |
| Ism Maf'ool | مُسْلَم | Peaceful (done-to) |

### Pattern 2: فَعَلَ / يَفْعِلُ (Past 'a', Present 'i')
| Form | Example | Meaning |
|------|---------|---------|
| Past | عَلِمَ | He knew |
| Present | يَعْلَمُ | He knows |
| Ism Fa'il | مُعْلِم | Teacher |
| Ism Maf'ool | مُعْلَم | Known |

### Pattern 3: فَعُلَ / يَفْعُلُ (Past 'u', Present 'u')
| Form | Example | Meaning |
|------|---------|---------|
| Past | جَهُدَ | He strove |
| Present | يَجْهُدُ | He strives |
| Ism Fa'il | مُجْهِد | Striver |
| Ism Maf'ool | مُجْهَد | Striven for |

## ⚡ The 2 Heavy Families (Taf'eel)

Used for intensifying or making verbs transitive.

### Pattern 4: فَعَّلَ / يُفَعِّلُ (Shadda on middle letter)
| Form | Example | Meaning |
|------|---------|---------|
| Past | عَلَّمَ | He taught |
| Present | يُعَلِّمُ | He teaches |
| Masdar | تَعْلِيم | Teaching |

### Pattern 5: فَاعَلَ / يُفَاعِلُ (Alif after first letter)
| Form | Example | Meaning |
|------|---------|---------|
| Past | سَأَلَ | He asked |
| Present | يُسَائِلُ | He asks |
| Masdar | مُسَاءَلَة | Asking |

## 🌙 The 3 Hamza Families (Af'ala)

## 🧠 Quick Memory Tricks

### The "U-I-A" Song (for vowel patterns)
```
U-AAA-U (يَفْعَلُ)
UUU-I-A (يَفْعِلُ)  
U-AAA-U (يَفْعُلُ)
```

### Shadda = Heavy
- فَعَّلَ has shadda → it's HEAVY/INTENSIVE
- Think: "I'm putting EXTRA effort (shadda) into teaching"

### Alif = Partnership
- فَاعَلَ has alif → MUTUAL action
- Think: "Alif connects two people doing action together"

### Hamza = Cause/Request
- أَفْعَلَ → CAUSES the action
- اسْتَفْعَلَ → REQUESTS the action

## 📱 How to Use Your Hifz PWA

### Setup:
1. Go to **Settings** → Tag the groups as "sarf"
2. Start with "The 3 Main Families" group
3. Use **TTS** to hear pronunciation
4. Swipe through cards during spare moments

### Daily Routine:
1. **Morning** (5 min): Review "memorized" cards
2. **Commute** (10 min): Work on "learning" cards  
3. **Evening** (5 min): Mark new cards as "learning"

### Progress Tracking:
- 🆕 = Not started (new pattern)
- 📖 = Learning (recognize but not fluent)
- ✅ = Memorized (can recite pattern + example instantly)

## 🎓 Advanced: Command Forms

### Command (أَمْر) - Drop the ي from present:
| Present | Command | Meaning |
|---------|---------|---------|
| يَكْتُبُ | اُكْتُبْ | Write! |
| يَدْخُلُ | اُدْخُلْ | Enter! |
| يَسْجُدُ | اسْجُدْ | Prostrate! |

### Forbidding (نَهْي) - لَا + jussive:
| Present | Forbidding | Meaning |
|---------|------------|---------|
| تَكْتُبُ | لا تَكْتُبْ | Don't write! |
| تَذْهَبُ | لا تَذْهَبْ | Don't go! |
